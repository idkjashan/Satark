## Compounding

**Plain definition:** compounding means your returns start earning their own returns, so growth speeds up over time instead of staying flat [C.6].

**Everyday analogy:** a snowball rolling downhill picks up more snow the longer it rolls, and grows faster as it goes — it is not adding the same amount of snow each turn.

**The most common first-timer misunderstanding:** thinking "1% a day is modest." It is not. At 1% a day, ₹10,000 compounds to roughly 12 times its value in a single year (about 250 trading days), and no one — registered or not — can guarantee a daily return like this, ever [C.5, C24, C39].

**Safety tip:** treat any fixed daily, weekly or monthly percentage promise as a certain warning sign, however small the number sounds. "Small and guaranteed" and "real investing" do not go together.
