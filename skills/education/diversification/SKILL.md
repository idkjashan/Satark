## Diversification

**Plain definition:** diversification means spreading money across unrelated assets, so that one failure doesn't sink the whole portfolio [C.6].

**Everyday analogy:** it is like splitting your luggage across two separate bags on a trip — if one bag is lost, you have not lost everything you packed.

**The most common first-timer misunderstanding:** believing "following several tips from one channel is diversified," because the stock names are different. It is not — all of those tips share the exact same source of risk: if that tip channel or "operator" is wrong, or is running a pump-and-dump, every one of those positions moves together [C.6].

**Safety tip:** real diversification means different, independent sources of risk — different asset types, sectors or fund managers — not merely different stock names from the same single tipster.
