## Nomination

**Plain definition:** nomination means naming who receives your demat or mutual-fund holdings after you die. Up to 10 nominees have been allowed per account since January 2025 [C.6].

**Everyday analogy:** it is like naming who holds the key to a locked box on your behalf — the key lets that person act quickly, but the box's contents still belong, by law, to your actual legal heirs.

**The most common first-timer misunderstanding:** believing "the nominee becomes the owner" of the holdings. That is not how it works — a nominee is understood to hold the assets as a trustee for the legal heirs, not as the new, outright owner [C.6].

**Safety tip:** keep your nomination details updated on every demat and mutual-fund account; it determines who can access your holdings quickly after your death, even though final ownership still follows succession law.
