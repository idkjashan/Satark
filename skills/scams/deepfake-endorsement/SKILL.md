## Deepfake endorsements by officials or celebrities (scam type T7)

How it unfolds: an AI-generated ("deepfake") video shows a public figure — a minister, the RBI governor, a business leader or a celebrity — endorsing an investment platform and promising a specific return. One documented example showed a fake Finance Minister promising a deposit would grow from ₹22,000 to ₹25.5 lakh a month [C27]. The video drives a sign-up link, and the money moves as a joining deposit.

**Machine-checkable flags:**
- A named public figure appears together with a specific promised return and a sign-up link.
- The claim is framed as a personal endorsement ("I use this app and it made me rich").

**No real public figure endorses a specific private investment platform.** Ministers, the RBI governor and well-known business leaders do not promote individual trading apps or schemes — any video that shows this is fabricated.

**What the user should do:**
- Check PIB (Press Information Bureau) Fact Check and the public figure's own verified account before believing any such video; PIB Fact Check has specifically debunked finance-minister deepfakes of this kind [PIB-1].
- Never sign up or deposit money based on a video alone, however real it looks or sounds.
- Report the video on PIB Fact Check's WhatsApp or web channel so others are warned.
- Remember that video and audio can both be convincingly faked — a familiar face or voice is not evidence of anything.
